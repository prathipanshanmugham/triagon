# Triagon ERP — web edition (v2.0)

Browser-based ERP for Triagon Ventures: a CEO report with a 0–100 business health score, master stock with automatic deduction (leather by sq ft, paint and dye by ml / L / g / kg), consumables and packaging materials, catalogue and per-channel listings, inventory in any unit (pieces, sq ft, metres…), purchasing and vendor bills, custom-size sales orders, returns and platform claims, finance and bank accounts, marketing offers, listing ratings, attendance with a check-in kiosk, HR and payroll, daily PDF reports, and an MCP endpoint for AI agents.

- **Host it**: see [DEPLOY.md](DEPLOY.md) — clone this repo on a Hostinger VPS and run one script (Docker + Caddy HTTPS). Live at https://triagonventures.cloud once deployed.
- **Run it on a PC to try** (Node.js 22.13 or newer):
  ```
  npm ci
  npm run build
  npm start            # http://localhost:8080
  ```
  Data goes to `./data` (set `DATA_DIR` to change). PDFs need Chromium: `npx playwright-core install chromium` once.
- **AI agents**: `POST /mcp` (Streamable HTTP, Bearer key) or `/mcp/<key>`; REST at `/api/v1/<collection>`. Create keys in *Users & permissions › AI & API access*. Master stock is the `masters` collection (`/api/v1/masters`): each master material with live on-hand, committed, available, days left and the SKUs that use it. Create and update change its details; quantities change only through stock movements (`moves`, a physical count uses `type: count` with `setTo`). Opening stock and unit are locked once an item has history, SKUs must be unique, and a second master with the same name and unit is refused. `import_master_stock` (`POST /api/v1/masters/import`, preview by default, `GET` lists candidates) organises existing catalogue items into Master stock without copying them or changing their stock.

Works on phones, tablets and PCs: on narrow screens wide tables turn into one card per row and line-item editors stack their fields.

**CEO report** (Overview › CEO report, CEO only by default; grant it per role in Users & permissions): 29 measures in 7 areas — sales growth, profitability, cash & collections, inventory health, customers & fulfilment, listings & purchasing, people. Each measure scores 0 at its *poor* mark and 100 at its *good* mark; areas average their measures and the overall score is the weighted average of areas with data. It lists what to fix first (ranked by points gained), a brand scorecard, a copyable summary and a PDF. Marks and weights are editable in *Scoring targets*.

**Consumables & packaging** (its own menu entry under Catalogue & stock): boxes for leather and paint, poly bags, bubble wrap, bottles, cello tape, stickers and other packing materials. Each size is its own entry (Poly bag 6 × 8 in and Poly bag 8 × 10 in are two), with length / width / height and thickness in mm, cm, inches, micron, gauge, ply or gsm, roll length for tape and bubble wrap, capacity for bottles, the supplier (a vendor or any name) and, optionally, the stock on hand in pieces, rolls, packs and so on. Search, filter by category, add, edit, delete (single or in bulk) and export to CSV; *Add another size* copies an entry's material and supplier. No costs are kept here. Categories are managed in the page's *Categories* tab: the CEO (or a role given *Manage packaging categories* in Roles & permissions) can add, rename and delete them and set what new items start as, the stock unit and an extra size field (roll length or capacity). Renaming keeps items in place; deleting asks where its items go. Anyone who can edit packaging can select items and *Move to category*; only the category changes, and a move that would make two identical entries in one category is refused. The API accepts a category by key or name and `describe_collection` lists the current ones.

**Master stock** (Inventory › Master stock): raw materials such as leather (sq ft), paint and dye (ml, L, g, kg) and thread (m) are *master materials*, with type, colour, grade and specification. Any product can take its stock from one or more masters (Catalogue › product › Stock source, or *Link* on a master to link many SKUs at once — amounts like “50 ml”, “100 g” or “12 x 18 in” are read from product names). Each unit sold then deducts its amount from the master: a fixed pack size, a piece size ((L × W × qty) ÷ 144 sq ft for inches), or the size on each order for cut-to-size products. Stock is worked out from the records by one engine shared by the app and the server (`shared/stock.js`), so re-imported orders and repeated status updates can never deduct twice. Marketplace sales take stock when placed, shipped (default) or delivered, and sales orders when confirmed, cut, ready or dispatched (default) — set in *Stock settings*; before that they are committed. Cancelled orders take nothing, good returns go back, damaged returns go to damaged stock. A physical count sets on-hand; anything dated before it is not deducted again. Order imports (Amazon / Flipkart reports or CSV) match by SKU, channel SKU or ASIN and update existing orders instead of adding them twice.

Folders: `server/` (Node, SQLite via `node:sqlite`, no other database to run), `client/` (the app; `client/build.js` bundles it), `shared/` (modules and permissions used by both), `deploy/` (install and update scripts).
